How to use

What you see

Blue grid: space, warped by the central mass; rs = Mass × 4.5. The cube always spans ±6 units — Grid nodes only changes how many lines fill it.

Clocks: flow of time at each point. Red = slow, blue = fast. Single plane shows one slice (sweep with Plane height); all nodes shows the full 3D time field.

Probe: released at rest on the +x face of the cube, it falls along the time-flow gradient. The probe trail and the spacetime diagram record its path.

Light: Emit fires one beam from every node on the +x face toward the mass. Beams follow exact Schwarzschild null geodesics (grid coordinates are isotropic: horizon at rs/4, photon sphere at 0.93 rs). Every beam with impact parameter b < 2.6 rs is captured (the black-hole shadow); beams just outside skim the photon sphere and exit sideways or backwards. At high Mass the shadow exceeds the cube face, so most beams are swallowed, as GR predicts.

Desktop

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. All controls are in the bottom-left box.

Quest

Left stick: turn / raise-lower the model. Right stick: turn / zoom. Grip on model: move it. Grip while pointing at the panel: pick the panel up. Trigger: press. A/X: drop probe. B/Y: reset. Click a stick: recenter. The yellow HELP button on the in-VR panel opens this text there.

Panel

Mass drives all physics. Ball size is visual only. Grid nodes: 5–15 per edge. Grid detail: segments per line — raise it if warped lines look angular.
